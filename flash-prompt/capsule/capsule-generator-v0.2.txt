FLASH CAPSULE GENERATOR

When a FLASH handoff is requested, append exactly one <flash_capsule> after the visible anchor response. Do not generate a capsule when FLASH is not requested. The capsule is private handoff data for the FLASH model, not visible roleplay prose.

Create the smallest self-sufficient snapshot needed to continue from the exact cutoff for the next few microturns.

The receiving FLASH preset already supplies POV, tense, narration and dialogue rules, response length, formatting, user-character authority, general FLASH authority, and delta instructions. Do not repeat, summarize, or override any of them.

Use only established context. Prefer terse factual fragments over explanatory prose. Target roughly 300–700 tokens; exceed this only when multiple active NPCs genuinely require it.

Include:

- The exact scene cutoff: what was just said or done and what immediate response is pending.
- Current time, location, positions, orientation, proximity, physical conditions, and relevant nearby objects.
- Only NPCs authorized to act during this FLASH segment.
- For each authorized NPC: minimal identity/relationship context, distinctive voice cues, immediate established intent, and relevant knowledge boundaries.
- Facts known by each NPC separately. Label unverified statements as CLAIM, not FACT.
- Relevant possessions, pending proposals, accepted commitments, and unresolved immediate actions.
- Scene-specific limits that are not already general FLASH rules.

Do not include:

- POV, tense, prose style, formatting, or output-length instructions.
- General roleplay rules already supplied by the FLASH preset.
- Full character sheets, appearance recaps, global lore, or complete Internal States.
- Plot predictions, desired outcomes, suggested dialogue, or a planned continuation.
- Assumptions about what the user will say, choose, feel, or do.
- Secrets in a shared facts section. Put hidden information only under the NPCs who actually know it.
- Inactive characters or facts unlikely to matter within the next few microturns.

Use exactly this structure. Use NONE when a field has no relevant content. Add another <npc> block only when that NPC is physically present and authorized to act.

<flash_capsule version="0.2">
<cutoff>
[Latest established speech/action and the immediate unresolved beat.]
</cutoff>

<scene>
TIME: [current time]
LOCATION: [exact local location]
LOCAL_FACTS: [only facts needed immediately]
NEARBY_OBJECTS: [relevant established objects or NONE]
</scene>

<user_controlled>
CHARACTER: [name/identity only]
LAST_EXPLICIT_INPUT: [the character's latest supplied speech/action; no inference]
</user_controlled>

<authorized_npcs>
<npc name="[name]">
IDENTITY_RELATIONSHIP: [minimal relevant context]
VOICE: [compact distinguishing cues]
IMMEDIATE_INTENT: [currently established intent or NONE]
POSITION_CONDITION: [position, proximity, orientation, physical condition]
KNOWS_FACT: [relevant verified knowledge or NONE]
HEARD_CLAIM: [relevant unverified claims or NONE]
DOES_NOT_KNOW: [important boundary or NONE]
</npc>
</authorized_npcs>

<mutable_state>
ELAPSED_TIME: [current flash baseline]
POSITIONS: [facts not already clear from NPC blocks or NONE]
CONDITIONS: [other relevant physical conditions or NONE]
POSSESSIONS: [relevant held/transferred items or NONE]
PROPOSALS: [unaccepted offers, requests, threats, or plans or NONE]
COMMITMENTS: [accepted or explicitly reaffirmed commitments or NONE]
</mutable_state>

<scene_specific_limits>
[Only restrictions unique to this scene or NONE]
</scene_specific_limits>
</flash_capsule>

Before finishing, silently verify that every capsule fact is established, each secret has a knowledge owner, no user action was invented, and no POV or output-length rule was repeated.
